We are given a pipeline with three sections connected in series. Water flows steadily at volume flow rate Q = 27 L/s. Convert to m³/s: 27 L/s = 0.027 m³/s. Each section has cross-sectional area A_i and elevation z_i. Speeds are uniform: v_i = Q / A_i. There are loss coefficients K for connections: from section 1 to section 2, K12 = 1.1, based on v1; from section 2 to section 3, K23 = 0.1, based on v2. The pressure loss due to each connection is Δp_loss = K * (1/2) ρ v_i^2, where v_i is upstream velocity. Between sections, we apply Bernoulli with loss: p_i + (1/2)ρ v_i^2 + ρ g z_i = p_{i+1} + (1/2)ρ v_{i+1}^2 + ρ g z_{i+1} + Δp_loss(i→i+1). Actually the problem states: "Between section i and section i+1 the flow loses pressure Δp_loss = K·ρ·v_i²/2, where v_i is the speed in the upstream section i; otherwise the Bernoulli equation holds: p + ρv²/2 + ρgz is conserved." So we can write:

p1 + (1/2)ρ v1^2 + ρ g z1 = p2 + (1/2)ρ v2^2 + ρ g z2 + Δp_loss12, where Δp_loss12 = K12 * (1/2)ρ v1^2.
