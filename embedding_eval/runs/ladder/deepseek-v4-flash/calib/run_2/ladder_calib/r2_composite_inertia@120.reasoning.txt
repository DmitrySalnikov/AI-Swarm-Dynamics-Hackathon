We have a composite rigid body consisting of 120 parts (point masses, rods, disks, rings, rectangular plates). We need to compute the total moment of inertia about an axis perpendicular to the xy-plane through the point (-2, -2). That is, we need the moment of inertia about the axis passing through (-2,-2) and parallel to the z-axis.

For each component, we compute its mass and its moment of inertia about the given axis. For extended bodies, we can use the parallel axis theorem: I = I_cm + m * d^2, where d is the distance from the component's center of mass to the axis point (-2,-2). For point masses, I = m * d^2 directly.

We need to be careful: For rods, they are thin uniform rods; the moment of inertia about an axis through its center of mass perpendicular to the rod (and to the plane, since the axis is perpendicular to the plane) is (1/12) m L^2. But note: The rod lies in the xy-plane, so the axis through its center of mass perpendicular to the plane is indeed the same direction (z-axis). So I_cm = mL^2/12.

For solid disks: I_cm = (1/2) m R^2.

For thin rings (hoops): I_cm = m R^2.

For rectangular plates: sides a and b, axis through center perpendicular to plane: I_cm = m (a^2 + b^2)/12.

All these are standard.

We must consider overlapping parts: The problem says "parts may overlap; they are all rigidly joined". So we just sum contributions.

We need to compute total mass and total moment of inertia about (-2,-2). The total moment of inertia is the sum of each component's I.

We have 120 items. We'll need to compute for each: mass, distance squared from (-2,-2) to the component's center of mass (or point location for point masses), and the component's own I_cm about its own center. Then add m*d^2 + I_cm.

We must be careful with the rods: For a rod, the center of mass is at its midpoint. So we need coordinates of the endpoints to find midpoint. For rectangular plates, center is given. For disks and rings, center is given. For point masses, location given.

Thus we can compute systematically.

Given the large number, we will need to compute sums. For each component i, we compute d_i^2 = (x_i - (-2))^2 + (y_i - (-2))^2 = (x_i+2)^2 + (y_i+2)^2.

Then I_i = I_cm_i + m_i * d_i^2.

We need to sum all I_i.
