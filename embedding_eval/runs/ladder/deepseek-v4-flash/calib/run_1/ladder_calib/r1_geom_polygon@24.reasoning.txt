We are given a list of 24 points with integer coordinates, in counter-clockwise order. We need to find the area of the polygon. Since coordinates are integers, the area can be computed using the shoelace formula. The area will be either integer or half-integer. We should compute the sum over edges of (x_i*y_{i+1} - x_{i+1}*y_i) and then take half of absolute value. Since order is CCW, the sum should be positive, so area = (1/2) * sum.
