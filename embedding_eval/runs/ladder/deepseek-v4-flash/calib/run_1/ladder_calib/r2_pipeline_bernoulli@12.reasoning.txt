So the pressure drop due to minor losses at the connections is accounted for. For each segment between sections i and i+1, we can apply the Bernoulli equation with loss:

p_i + (1/2)ρ v_i² + ρ g z_i = p_{i+1} + (1/2)ρ v_{i+1}^2 + ρ g z_{i+1} + Δp_loss_i

p_i + (1/2)ρ v_i² + ρ g z_i = p_{i+1} + (1/2)ρ v_{i+1}^2 + ρ g z_{i+1} + K_i * (1/2)ρ v_i²
