For k from 1 to n-1, for i,j > k, set a_{ij}^{(k)} = (a_{ij}^{(k-1)} a_{kk}^{(k-1)} - a_{ik}^{(k-1)} a_{kj}^{(k-1)}) / (a_{k-1,k-1}^{(k-2)}), with the convention that a_{0,0}^{( -1)} = 1. Then the determinant is a_{nn}^{(n-1)}.
