We are given a pipeline with 6 sections connected in series. Water flows steadily at volume flow rate Q = 19 L/s = 0.019 m³/s (since 1 L = 0.001 m³). Density ρ = 1000 kg/m³, g = 9.8 m/s².

For each section i, we have cross-sectional area A_i (given in cm², convert to m²), and centreline elevation z_i (in m). The flow velocity in each section is v_i = Q / A_i.

Between section i and i+1, there is a loss coefficient K_i (given for connection from section i to i+1). The pressure loss due to this connection is Δp_loss,i = K_i * (ρ * v_i² / 2), where v_i is the velocity in the upstream section i (the section before the junction). The problem says: "Between section i and section i+1 the flow loses pressure Δp_loss = K·ρ·v_i²/2, where v_i is the speed in the upstream section i; otherwise the Bernoulli equation holds: p + ρv²/2 + ρgz is conserved." So we can apply Bernoulli equation from one section to the next, but with a head loss term (pressure loss). So for flow from section i to i+1, we have:

p_i + (1/2)ρ v_i² + ρ g z_i = p_{i+1} + (1/2)ρ v_{i+1}^2 + ρ g z_{i+1} + Δp_loss,i

But careful: The loss term is usually added on the right as a loss, so total pressure decreases. So:

p_i + (1/2)ρ v_i² + ρ g z_i = p_{i+1} + (1/2)ρ v_{i+1}^2 + ρ g z_{i+1} + K_i * (1/2)ρ v_i²

Thus rearranged:

p_{i+1} = p_i + (1/2)ρ (v_i² - v_{i+1}^2) + ρ g (z_i - z_{i+1}) - K_i * (1/2)ρ v_i²

Or equivalently, p_{i+1} = p_i + (1/2)ρ (v_i² (1 - K_i) - v_{i+1}^2) + ρ g (z_i - z_{i+1}).
