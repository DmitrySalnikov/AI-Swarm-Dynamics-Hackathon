The flow is steady, incompressible, with density ρ = 1000 kg/m³, volume flow rate Q = 20 L/s = 0.020 m³/s. Use g = 9.8 m/s². Absolute pressure in section 1 is p1 = 265 kPa. p1/ρ + v1^2/2 + g z1 = p6/ρ + v6^2/2 + g z6 + (sum of losses from connections 1->2, 2->3, ..., 5->6)
