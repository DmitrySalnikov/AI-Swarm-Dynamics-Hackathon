However, the minimal polynomial could be (x-1)^k for some k between 1 and 8. We need to find the minimal polynomial of A, i.e., the monic polynomial of least degree such that m(A)=0. Since eigenvalue is only 1, minimal polynomial is (x-1)^d where d is the size of the largest Jordan block for eigenvalue 1.

Thus we need to find the index of nilpotency of (A - I). That is, find the smallest d such that (A-I)^d = 0. Then the minimal polynomial is (x-1)^d.
