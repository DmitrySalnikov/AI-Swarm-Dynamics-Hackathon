The moment of inertia about a perpendicular axis through a given point for a planar body is the sum of moments of inertia of each part about that axis, using parallel axis theorem.

For each part, we need its mass, its center of mass (or location), and its moment of inertia about its own center of mass (for the perpendicular axis through its CM).
