General formulas:

Doppler effect for sound: When source and observer move relative to medium (air), the observed frequency f' is given by:
f' = f * (v ± v_o) / (v ∓ v_s)
where v is speed of sound, v_o is speed of observer relative to medium, v_s is speed of source relative to medium. Signs: For observer moving towards source, use + in numerator; away, use -. For source moving towards observer, use - in denominator; away, use +. (Typical convention: f' = f * (v + v_o)/(v - v_s) if both moving towards each other, with signs chosen accordingly.)
