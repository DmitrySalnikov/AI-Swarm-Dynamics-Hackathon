Bareiss algorithm for matrix A (n x n) proceeds by:

For k from 1 to n-1:
  for i from k+1 to n:
    for j from k+1 to n:
      a_{i,j} := (a_{i,j} * a_{k,k} - a_{i,k} * a_{k,j}) / (previous pivot)
where previous pivot is a_{k-1,k-1} for k>1, and for k=1, divisor is 1. This yields exact integers if the matrix is integer. At the end, det = a_{n,n} after final step (or the last pivot). (But careful: The final entry after the last iteration is the determinant.) Let's apply to D.
