The SNF is a diagonal matrix diag(d1,...,d_n) where d_i are nonnegative integers, d_i divides d_{i+1} for i=1,...,n-1, and zeros come last. The diagonal entries are the invariant factors. The SNF is obtained by performing elementary row and column operations (invertible over Z). We need to compute d1,...,d6.
