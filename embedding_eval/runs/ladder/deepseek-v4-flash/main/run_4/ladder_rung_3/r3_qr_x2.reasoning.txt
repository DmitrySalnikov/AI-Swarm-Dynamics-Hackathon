Interpretation: For each column j, the diagonal entry R_jj is the norm of the projection of the j-th column of A onto the orthogonal complement of the span of previous columns (i.e., the norm of the vector after Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization). Specifically, in Gram-Schmidt, we compute:

Define u1 = a1, r11 = ||u1||, q1 = u1 / r11.
Then for j=2..6: u_j = a_j - sum_{i=1}^{j-1} (q_i^T a_j) q_i, r_jj = ||u_j||, q_j = u_j / r_jj.

Thus R_jj = ||u_j||, so R_jj^2 = ||u_j||^2 = the squared length of the component of a_j orthogonal to previous columns.
