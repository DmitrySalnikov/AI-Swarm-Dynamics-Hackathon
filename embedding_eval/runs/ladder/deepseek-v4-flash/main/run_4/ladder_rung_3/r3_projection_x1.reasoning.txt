We are asked to compute the orthogonal projection of vector b onto subspace W spanned by six linearly independent vectors w1,...,w6 in R^8 with standard dot product. The vectors are given. The subspace W is 6-dimensional. We need to find the projection of b onto W, i.e., the vector in W that is closest to b. Since the vectors are linearly independent, they form a basis for W. We can compute projection using the formula: proj_W(b) = A (A^T A)^{-1} A^T b, where A is the 8x6 matrix whose columns are the basis vectors. Or we can use Gram-Schmidt to find an orthonormal basis for W and then project. However, because the vectors are given directly, we could compute the coefficients by solving the normal equations. Let's do that.

Let A = [w1 w2 w3 w4 w5 w6] as columns. Then the projection is A * x, where x = (A^T A)^{-1} A^T b.

We need to compute A^T A (6x6 matrix) and A^T b (6-vector). Then solve for x. Then compute A x. Since the entries may become fractions, careful arithmetic needed. We can compute by hand or systematically.
