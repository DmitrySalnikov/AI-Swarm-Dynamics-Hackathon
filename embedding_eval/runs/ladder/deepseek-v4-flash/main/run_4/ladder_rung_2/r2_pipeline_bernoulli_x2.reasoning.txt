Between section i and i+1, there is a loss coefficient K_i (given for connection from section i to i+1). The loss is Δp_loss = K * (ρ * v_i^2 / 2), where v_i is the velocity in the upstream section (i). The Bernoulli equation with losses: p_i + (1/2)ρ v_i^2 + ρ g z_i = p_{i+1} + (1/2)ρ v_{i+1}^2 + ρ g z_{i+1} + Δp_loss (the loss term is added to the right side as an extra energy loss). Actually, Bernoulli's equation with loss: p1 + 1/2 ρ v1^2 + ρ g z1 = p2 + 1/2 ρ v2^2 + ρ g z2 + Δp_loss, where Δp_loss is the pressure drop due to friction etc. So we have: p_i + ½ρv_i^2 + ρgz_i = p_{i+1} + ½ρv_{i+1}^2 + ρg z_{i+1} + K_i * (½ρv_i^2).
