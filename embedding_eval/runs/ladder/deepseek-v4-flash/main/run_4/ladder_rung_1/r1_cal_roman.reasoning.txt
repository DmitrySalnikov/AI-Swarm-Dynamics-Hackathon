We are given 160 Roman calendar dates and need to convert each to modern day-month format (DD.MM). The year is common (non-leap) and uses Julian/Gregorian month lengths. We need to follow the rules. The months have named days: Kalends (1st), Nones (5th or 7th depending on month), Ides (13th or 15th). For months with 31 days (March, May, July, October) the Nones are on the 7th and Ides on the 15th; for others (30 or 28 days) Nones on 5th, Ides on 13th. January and February have 31 and 28 days respectively, but note: January Nones on 5th, Ides on 13th; February Nones on 5th, Ides on 13th. So the rule: In March, May, July, October, Nones=7, Ides=15; in all others, Nones=5, Ides=13.

Conversion: "Kalendis <month>" = 1st of that month. "Nonis <month>" = Nones day of that month (i.e. 5th or 7th). "Idibus <month>" = Ides day (13th or 15th). "pridie <named day>" = day before that named day (i.e., named day minus 1). "ante diem N Kalendas/Nonas/Idus <month>" = count backwards inclusively from that named day; so date = named day - (N-1). For example, "ante diem IV Nonas Septembres": N=4, named day = Nonas Septembres = September Nones = 5th (since September Nones on 5th). So date = 5 - (4-1) = 5 - 3 = 2nd September. But careful: "ante diem" counts backwards inclusively: the named day is day 1, so "ante diem II" is the day before (pridie). So formula: day = (named day) - (N - 1). For "Kalendas <month>" it refers to the Kalends of that month, i.e., the 1st of that month, but note: dates after the Ides of a month count towards Kalends of the next month. So if we have "ante diem N Kalendas <month>", it means we count backwards from the 1st of that month, so the date falls in the previous month. So we need to compute the day of the previous month.

Similarly, "pridie Kalendas <month>" = day before the Kalends = last day of previous month.

Also note: "Nonas <month>" and "Idus <month>" refer to the Nones and Ides of that month, so the date is in that month. For "ante diem N Nonas <month>" and "ante diem N Idus <month>", it's also in that month.
