We are given a pipeline of 6 sections connected in series. Water flows steadily at volume flow rate Q = 7 L/s = 0.007 m³/s. Each section has a cross-sectional area A_i and elevation z_i. The loss coefficients for each connection from section i to i+1 are given as K_i (where i from 1 to 5). The loss pressure drop is Δp_loss = K * (ρ * v_i² / 2), where v_i is the speed in the upstream section i. The Bernoulli equation with losses: between section i and i+1, we have:

p_i + (1/2)ρ v_i^2 + ρ g z_i = p_{i+1} + (1/2)ρ v_{i+1}^2 + ρ g z_{i+1} + Δp_loss_i

where Δp_loss_i = K_i * (1/2)ρ v_i^2.

Thus:

p_i + (1/2)ρ v_i^2 + ρ g z_i = p_{i+1} + (1/2)ρ v_{i+1}^2 + ρ g z_{i+1} + K_i * (1/2)ρ v_i^2

Rearranging:

p_{i+1} = p_i + (1/2)ρ v_i^2 - (1/2)ρ v_{i+1}^2 + ρ g (z_i - z_{i+1}) - K_i * (1/2)ρ v_i^2

Simplify:

p_{i+1} = p_i + (1 - K_i) * (1/2)ρ v_i^2 - (1/2)ρ v_{i+1}^2 + ρ g (z_i - z_{i+1})
