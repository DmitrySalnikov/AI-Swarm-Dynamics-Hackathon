Let's denote:

- For a downhill incline: the block moves down the incline, so gravity does positive work: m g * sin(θ) * L (component along the incline direction). Friction does negative work: -μ m g cos(θ) * L. So net work = m g L (sinθ - μ cosθ). So ΔKE = m g L (sinθ - μ cosθ).
