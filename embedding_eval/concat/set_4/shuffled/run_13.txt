\[
\frac{m^2 n}{3^m(n3^m+m3^n)}

\[
S=\sum_{m,n\ge 1}\frac{a_m^2a_n}{a_m+a_n}.

\[

\]

\[
\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{k}{3^k}

\]
Thus
\[
S=\frac12\left(\frac34\right)^2
