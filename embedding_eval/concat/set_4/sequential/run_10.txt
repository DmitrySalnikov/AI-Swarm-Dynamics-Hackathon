The second equation represents the perpendicular bisector of the points \(4+k\) and \(k+3i\).  
Let \(z=x+iy\). Then
\[
|z-(4+k)|=|z-(k+3i)|
\]
becomes
\[
(x-k-4)^2+y^2=(x-k)^2+(y-3)^2
\]
which simplifies to
\[
8x-6y=8k+7.
\]
So the line is
\[
8x-6y-(8k+7)=0.
\]

The circle is centered at \((25,20)\) with radius \(5\). For exactly one solution, the line must be tangent to the circle, so the distance from \((25,20)\) to the line equals \(5\):
\[
\frac{|8(25)-6(20)-(8k+7)|}{\sqrt{8^2+(-6)^2}}=5.
\]
\[
\frac{|73-8k|}{10}=5
\]
\[
|73-8k|=50.
\]
Thus
\[
73-8k=50 \Rightarrow k=\frac{23}{8},
\]
or
\[
73-8k=-50 \Rightarrow k=\frac{123}{8}.
\]
The sum of all possible values of \(k\) is
\[
\frac{23}{8}+\frac{123}{8}=\frac{146}{8}=\frac{73}{4}.
\]
So \(m=73,\ n=4\), and
\[
m+n=77.
